Frequently asked questions

Is the review really free?

Yes. There's no charge and no obligation. If you choose a new policy, our agents are paid a commission by the insurance company.

How is Lifestone Life connected to Lifestone Settlements?

Lifestone Life is the licensed insurance agency affiliated with Lifestone Settlements. If your policy wasn't a fit for a settlement, our agency may still be able to help with your coverage.

Will I qualify for new coverage?

It depends on your age, health and the coverage you need. All coverage is subject to underwriting and approval by the insurance company. Your agent will tell you honestly what's realistic.

My conversion deadline passed. Is it too late?

Not necessarily. You may still qualify for a new term or permanent policy through regular underwriting. A review will show what's available.

Do I have to cancel my current policy?

No. Never cancel an existing policy until a new one is approved and in force. Your agent will explain the pros and cons of keeping or replacing what you have.

Will I need a medical exam?

Some policies require one and some don't. It depends on your age, the carrier and the amount of coverage. Your agent will explain what to expect up front.
