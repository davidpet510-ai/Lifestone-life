Your coverage options

Three kinds of life insurance. One right fit for you.

Here’s what each one does well, where it falls short and how they compare. Not sure? Your agent will help you choose.

Most affordable

Term life

Big protection for the years your family needs it most.

Lasts
10, 15, 20 or 30 years
Cost
$$$
Cash value
None

Best forReplacing your income, paying off a mortgage, raising kids

Benefits

  • The lowest price for the most coverage
  • Premium stays the same for the whole term
  • Many policies can convert to permanent coverage later without a new medical exam

Downsides

  • Coverage ends when the term does, with no payout if you outlive it
  • No savings or cash value
  • Buying new coverage later costs more as you age

Lasts

Best forLeaving a legacy, final expenses, estate planning

Benefits

  • Covers you for life as long as premiums are paid
  • Premiums and payout are locked in and guaranteed
  • Builds cash value at a guaranteed rate that you can borrow against

Downsides

  • Costs much more than term for the same payout
  • Cash value grows slowly in the first years
  • Canceling early can mean getting back less than you paid

How getting covered works

From your first question to a policy in hand, in six steps.

Here’s exactly what happens, how long each step takes and what you’ll need, so there are no surprises.

  2. Talk with a licensed agent

01
Step 1 About 60 seconds

Answer a few quick questions

Tell us what you want to protect, your age and roughly how much coverage you have in mind.

  • No Social Security number, medical records or payment needed
  • Pick a time that works for a call with your agent
  • Free, with no obligation to buy anything

What you’ll needJust a few minutes

02
Step 2 15–20 minute call

Talk with a licensed agent

A real person walks through your situation and helps you figure out how much coverage you need, and for how long.

  • We look at your income, mortgage and debts, your kids’ ages and any coverage you already have, including through work
  • Term and permanent coverage explained in plain English
  • A recommended amount and length that fits your budget

What you’ll needA rough idea of your income, debts and existing policies

03
Step 3 Same call or the next day

Compare quotes side by side

We shop multiple carriers and lay your best options out next to each other, so you can see exactly what you’d pay and get.

  • Price, length of coverage and conversion options compared
  • Optional extras explained, like waiver of premium or early access to benefits if you become terminally ill
  • An honest recommendation — and the choice stays yours

What you’ll needNothing — we do the shopping

04
Step 4 About 20–30 minutes

Apply with your agent

Your agent fills out the application with you over the phone, so nothing gets missed and nothing is confusing.

  • Questions about your health history, medications, lifestyle and family history
  • You name your beneficiaries — the people who receive the money
  • Sign electronically; no printing or mailing

What you’ll needYour ID, your doctors’ names, a list of medications and your beneficiaries’ details

05
Step 5 A few days to several weeks

The carrier reviews your application

This step is called underwriting. The insurance company confirms your information to set your final price.

  • They may check prescription history, medical records, driving record and the MIB (an insurance information database)
  • Some people qualify without a medical exam; others have a free 30-minute exam at home or work
  • We track your application and keep you updated, so you never have to chase anyone

What you’ll needPossibly a short, free exam: height, weight, blood pressure and a blood and urine sample

06
Step 6 Final step

You’re approved and covered

The carrier approves your application and sets your rate. We go over the final offer with you before anything is final.

  • Accept the offer or walk away — still no obligation
  • Coverage starts once your policy is issued and your first premium is paid
  • A free-look period, usually 10 to 30 days depending on your state, lets you cancel for a full refund

After thatWe stay your agent for annual reviews and beneficiary updates

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much life insurance do I need?

A common starting point is enough to replace several years of income, plus your mortgage and other debts, plus future costs like college. Your agent will walk through the numbers with you so you don't buy too little or too much.

Does it cost anything to get a quote?

No. Quotes and advice are free with no obligation. If you choose a policy, our agents are paid a commission by the insurance company.

Will I need a medical exam?

Some policies require one and some don't. It depends on your age, the carrier and the amount of coverage. Your agent will tell you what to expect up front.

I already have a policy. Can you still help?

Yes. We can add coverage or review what you have and tell you honestly whether it still fits. Never cancel an existing policy until a new one is approved and in force. Get a free policy review →
