Frequently asked questions

How much life insurance do I need?

A common starting point is enough to replace several years of income, plus your mortgage and other debts, plus future costs like college. Your agent will walk through the numbers with you so you don't buy too little or too much.

Is term or permanent life insurance better?

It depends on your goal. Term is usually the most affordable way to cover a specific period, like raising kids or paying off a mortgage. Permanent coverage is built to last your whole life and is often used for legacy and estate planning. Many people use both.

Does it cost anything to get a quote?

No. Quotes and advice are free with no obligation. If you choose a policy, our agents are paid a commission by the insurance company.

Will I need a medical exam?

Some policies require one and some don't. It depends on your age, the carrier and the amount of coverage. Your agent will tell you what to expect up front.

How long does it take to get covered?

It varies by carrier and policy. Some approvals take days and others take several weeks. We handle the paperwork and keep you updated the whole way.

I already have a policy. Can you still help?

Yes. We can add coverage or review what you have and tell you honestly whether it still fits. Never cancel an existing policy until a new one is approved and in force. Get a free policy review →
